Re: LAMA T-REx accuracy discrepancy

Hey, I looked into why our results differ so much (99.7% vs. 67%) and found a data
leakage issue in the evaluation setup.

THE PROBLEM

The HuggingFace `lama/trex` dataset expands every fact into all of its supporting
Wikipedia sentences -- so a single fact like "Prime Minister of Canada -> Canada" can
appear as hundreds of separate examples. When you load train[:34000] and do a random
train_test_split, different sentences for the same facts end up in both train and eval.
We verified this: 92.3% of eval facts were already seen during training.

After 3 epochs on ~3,000 repeated facts, the model simply memorizes them and scores
near-perfectly on eval -- but it's not demonstrating factual generalization, it's an
open-book exam.

HOW TO FIX IT

Two options:

1. Use the pre-filtered LAMA benchmark files (from the original LAMA repo). These
   already have exactly one sentence per fact, and the facts are unique -- no leakage
   possible.

2. De-duplicate before splitting: group by (sub_uri, obj_uri, predicate_id), keep one
   sentence per fact, then split. This gives a fair held-out set of facts the model
   never saw during training.

With either fix, expect accuracy to drop significantly -- our result on the clean
setup is ~67%.
